DIGITAL RECEPTIONIST — MASTER DEMO v1

This is a reusable static kiosk template demonstrated as a fictional physiotherapy clinic.

CUSTOMISE A NEW CLIENT
1. Replace welcome-screen.png with the client's approved portrait welcome image.
2. Edit config.js only: business name, colours, contact details, booking URL, hours, services, arrival info, quick questions and approved Q&A.
3. Keep index.html, styles.css and app.js as the shared core wherever possible.
4. Test every button/link on the actual iPad before deployment.

IMPORTANT
- All Ardee Physio Clinic details in this demo are fictional/example content.
- The medical receptionist is intentionally limited to administrative information and approved FAQs. It does not diagnose or give personal medical advice.
- The booking QR area is a visual placeholder in this demo. For a real client, generate/bundle a QR image for their booking URL or add an approved QR-generation method.
- Static hosting can be used for the kiosk. Third-party booking, payment, messaging and other services may have their own fees/terms.
